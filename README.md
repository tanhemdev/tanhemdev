**things i've shipped:**

[**tan-1**](https://github.com/tanhemdev/tan1-health): my twin sister tanisha was diagnosed with type 1 diabetes. i interviewed 80+ people and built a glucose risk prediction system (92% accuracy across 10K+ readings) that turns CGM numbers into plain-english caregiver alerts, plus a community where people living with diabetes find each other. react native + scikit-learn. presented at 2 health-tech conferences.

[**curb**](https://github.com/tanhemdev/curb): watched furniture pile up on the curb during berkeley move-out, then watched the next class buy the same stuff at target. peer marketplace that diverted 800+ items from landfills in our pilot. the average student generates 640 lbs of waste/year.

[**neurolearner**](https://github.com/tanhemdev/neurolearner): 1 in 5 US kids have learning differences and 18% drop out. adaptive platform that adjusts pacing and feedback to each child's cognitive profile. 35% improvement in task completion across 15 pilot students.

[**lyric-sync**](https://github.com/tanhemdev/lyric-sync): bad bunny has 103M+ spotify listeners, 77M in the US. most don't speak spanish. real-time synced translations that preserve the poetry across 50+ songs.
