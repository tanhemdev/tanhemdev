01 About
I turn data into products people actually use. First-gen student. Cognitive scientist. I let the numbers tell me what to build, then I build it until it works for everyone.

I study cognitive science and data science at UC Berkeley because good products start with understanding people and end with data-driven decisions. I've built an AI diabetes alert system that started with my twin sister's diagnosis, a student marketplace where 127 items were listed in its first move-out pilot, and a lyric engine that makes non-English music actually make sense.

I care a lot about accessibility and using data to surface insights for people who usually get left out. That's what led me to building gesture-based games for kids with motor disabilities at a clinic in Mumbai, and what drives most of what I make.

LyricSync: Cultural Context Engine

Founder / Builder

Problem: 128M people watched Bad Bunny's Super Bowl halftime, almost entirely in Spanish. Most couldn't follow the words, and word-for-word translation turns slang into nonsense. The music hits. The meaning doesn't.

What I built: A Spotify-connected lyric sync engine: synced lyrics from LRCLIB, translated for meaning by Claude, with notes on slang and double meanings. Poetry stays poetry. Slang stays slang.
