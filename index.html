01 About
I turn data into products people actually use. First-gen student. Cognitive scientist. I let the numbers tell me what to build, then I build it until it works for everyone.

I study cognitive science and data science at UC Berkeley because good products start with understanding people and end with data-driven decisions. I've shipped an AI diabetes platform backed by 10K+ glucose readings for my twin sister's family, a marketplace that kept 127 items out of landfills, and a lyric engine that makes non-English music actually make sense.

I care a lot about accessibility and using data to surface insights for people who usually get left out. That's what led me to building gesture-based games for kids with motor disabilities at a clinic in Mumbai, and what drives most of what I make.
