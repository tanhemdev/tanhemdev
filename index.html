01 About
I love to build, to take an idea and bring it to life. What drives me is bigger than any one product: I want to change how people interact with the systems they depend on most.

I'm a cognitive science and data science student at UC Berkeley, building at the intersection of product, health, and AI. I've shipped real products — from an AI diabetes platform pitched to investors, to marketplace tools that cut move-out waste on campus.

When I'm not building, I'm leading teams at the Fung Institute, captaining the Table Tennis Club, or running consulting sprints with nonprofits through Roots Consulting.
